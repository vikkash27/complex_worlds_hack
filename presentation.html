The Problem

Physical AI agents post-SWE struggle with the real world — tasks that demand memory, recovery, and multi-step reasoning over hundreds of timesteps.

📉

Sparse Reward Problem

Agents get rewarded only at the end — no signal for hundreds of steps.

🧠

Long-Horizon Credit Assignment

Across 20+ macro-steps, which action caused the failure? Nobody knows.

⚠️

No Recovery from Disturbances

When something gets knocked mid-task, agents have no recovery strategy.

🔬

Benchmarks Don't Test This

No standard eval exists for physical-AI with verifiable long-horizon rollouts.

Our Solution

An end-to-end OpenReward environment with dense Gemini-scored rewards at every semantic subgoal boundary — making long-horizon credit assignment tractable.
